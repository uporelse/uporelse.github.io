Honest Hire Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Honest Hire is a services marketplace app for iPhone by Ironclad Productions. It helps US small businesses find Ukrainian freelancers for development, design and AI automation work. Verification is based on paid buyer trials. There are no ads in the app.

What we (Ironclad Productions) collect

On the current TestFlight / demo build, almost everything stays on your iPhone. Sample talent profiles, trial records, messages and demo order references are stored in on-device storage only. There is no production backend yet, and we do not run analytics SDKs in this build.

  • On your device: app state such as requests, quotes, messages, saved talent and trial progress (AsyncStorage).
  • If you email us: your email address and message, used only to reply, then deleted when no longer needed.

Accounts and payments

Live accounts, identity checks and real payments are not enabled in this build. Demo checkout shows a breakdown and a DEMO-… reference only. Apple In-App Purchases are not used for project payments. When a live version ships, this page will be updated before any new data is collected.

Children

Honest Hire is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date before any change in how the app handles data.
