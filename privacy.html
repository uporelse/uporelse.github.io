Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Up or Else ("the app", "we") is an iPhone alarm app. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: your alarms, wake-up data, sleep data and sound clips stay on your iPhone. We do not run a server. The only things that leave the device are your App Store subscription status (RevenueCat), anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck) that you can turn off, and, only if you turn on the weather, either an approximate location or the city you typed, sent to Apple to get the forecast.

Summary

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, alarms, sleep data or anything you type. Audio is never uploaded by the app; a sound clip leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself (see "Sharing"). We have no servers that receive your data.
  • No account or sign-up.
  • Anonymous usage statistics only (TelemetryDeck), which you can turn off in Settings → Anonymous usage stats. No advertising or tracking SDKs, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no App Tracking Transparency prompt. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No ads, ever.

Data stored only on your device

The app stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Your alarms and their settings (time, repeat days, label, sound, volume, mission).
  • Your wake-up log used for the Progress tab: when each alarm was scheduled and first rang, when you completed the mission, snooze count, wrong answers, whether the easy fallback was used, and the result of the "Are you really up?" check.
  • Your wake-up streak and streak freezes.
  • Your sleep tracker nights and short sound clips (see "Sleep tracker and microphone" below).
  • The barcode or QR code value you register for the Scan mission (the camera image itself is not saved).
  • App preferences (for example, whether you finished onboarding, and which Morning card rows you turned on).
  • A small copy of your next alarm times, labels and streak in an on-device container shared with Up or Else's Home Screen / Lock Screen widget and Siri shortcuts, so they can show and change your alarms. It never leaves your device.

You can delete all of it anytime in Settings → Delete local data, or by deleting the app.

Permissions (all optional, asked only when you turn on the feature)

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): to schedule alarms that ring even on Silent.
  • Notifications: only for one reminder 2 days before your free trial ends, if you turn it on, and the optional Sunday recap of your own alarm stats, which you can turn off in Settings. No marketing notifications.
  • Motion: read live on the device for the Shake mission; not stored.
  • Camera: used live on the device to read the barcode or QR code for the Scan mission; no photos or video are saved or sent.
  • Microphone (optional, sleep tracker): used only while you run the sleep tracker. Sound is analysed live on the device, and short clips are saved only on your iPhone. See below.
  • Calendar (optional, Morning card): the app reads your first event of the day on the device to show it after your mission. Calendar data is never stored by the app or sent anywhere.
  • Approximate location or a typed city (optional, weather): see "Weather and location" below.
  • Siri & Shortcuts: voice requests are handled by Apple; the app only receives the resulting action (for example, "set an alarm for 7:00").

Sleep tracker and microphone

The sleep tracker is optional and only runs when you start it. While it runs, the app listens through the microphone and analyses the sound live on your iPhone to estimate noise events such as snoring, talking, coughing or loud noises. The app keeps a per-minute loudness line and a list of detected events (type and time), stored on your device for up to your last 60 nights.

Sound clips: when a sound is detected, the app saves a short clip (about 8–10 seconds) so you can play it back in the morning. Clips are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and are excluded from iCloud backup. They are never uploaded or sent to us, to analytics, or to anyone else. The only way a clip leaves your iPhone is if you tap Share on it yourself (see "Sharing"). At most about 30 clips are saved per night, and clips are deleted automatically after 7 days.

  • You can play or delete one clip, all clips of a night, or all clips.
  • Settings → Delete local data deletes all clips together with your other data.
  • Turn off Save sound clips in Settings (on by default) to stop saving clips; the live analysis still happens on the device.

You can also delete one night or all nights from the sleep report. The sleep tracker is a wellness feature, not a medical device, and its labels are best guesses.

Weather and location

Weather is off until you turn it on (Settings → Morning card → Weather). When it is on, today's weather is shown on the alarm (ring) screen and on the Morning card after you stop your alarm. You choose where the forecast is for in Settings → Weather:

  • My location: only if you allow it, the app uses your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km), only while the app is open ("While Using the App"; never in the background). The rounded location is sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) only to get today's forecast.
  • A city: if you type a city instead, the city name is sent to Apple to look up the place, and its rounded coordinates are sent to Apple Weather to get the forecast. No device location is used, and the app does not ask for location permission.

Apple receives this information to return the forecast, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive or store your location or city. Auto theme: while the weather is on, the same rounded place (your weather area or typed city, rounded further to about 10 km) is used on your iPhone to work out sunrise and sunset, so Settings → Appearance → Auto switches between light and dark at the right time. This is calculated on the device; nothing extra is sent, and the theme never asks for or reads your location itself. With the weather off, Auto switches at 07:00 and 19:00.

You can turn the weather off at any time in Settings → Morning card → Weather, and remove location access in iOS Settings.

On-device AI

Where available, fresh daily riddles and phrases are generated by Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). Prompts and results never leave your device.

Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck)

To learn which parts of the app work (for example, whether people finish onboarding or which paywall layout is clearer), the app sends a small number of anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Each event contains only:

  • the event name, from a fixed list (for example: app opened, onboarding step reached, paywall shown and which layout, trial started, purchase failed or cancelled, purchases restored, alarm saved, wake-up mission completed, share card created or shared);
  • a few fixed values describing it (for example: which plan, which mission type, which paywall layout, yes/no flags);
  • the app version and a random identifier created on your device when the app is first used, plus a random identifier for the current app session.

It never contains your name, email, alarm labels, phrases, scan codes, times, calendar, location, sleep data, sound clips or other audio, health data, the advertising identifier (IDFA) or anything you type. The random identifier is not derived from your device or from you; TelemetryDeck hashes it again before storing it, and it is reset when you use Settings → Delete local data. We do not combine this data with other data, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. Turn it off anytime in Settings → Anonymous usage stats. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy (https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are purchased and billed through Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment details. To check subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, which receives an anonymous app-specific identifier and App Store purchase information — not your name, email, or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Sharing

If you tap Share on a streak card, the image (with the app name and its App Store link) is created on your device and shared only through the iOS share sheet to the app you choose. Nothing is ever posted automatically. The usage statistics only record that a card was created or shared, never the image or where it went.

Sharing a sound clip: in the morning sleep report you can tap Share on a saved clip to make a picture card (the sound's type, such as "Snore", its time, a loudness wave, and the app name and icon) or to share the sound file itself. This happens only when you tap Share, and every time the app first warns you that the clip may contain your voice or the voices of other people nearby; Cancel is the default. The card or file is created on your iPhone and goes only through the iOS share sheet to the app or person you choose; we do not upload it or receive it. Once shared, a copy is handled by the app you chose under its own privacy policy, and deleting the clip in Up or Else does not delete that copy. Your clips themselves stay on your iPhone; delete them anytime in the report or with Settings → Delete all clips. Sharing is optional; after your first share, the app shows a small cosmetic badge on your Progress tab, which is stored on your iPhone and does not unlock or change anything.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we will delete it on request.

What personal data is processed, and why

  • Subscription status (RevenueCat): an anonymous app-specific user ID and App Store purchase information (which subscription, when it started, renews or ends, and whether it is a free trial). We use it only to provide the subscription you bought: unlocking the app, restoring purchases and checking whether your subscription is active.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck): event names with fixed values, the app version and a random per-install identifier (see above). We use them to understand and improve the app. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Anonymous usage stats.
  • Everything else stays on your iPhone (alarms, wake-up log, streak, sleep tracker data and sound clips, calendar event, scan code, preferences). It is not sent to us, and we do not process it on any server.

Service providers and where data is processed

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) processes the subscription data above on our behalf, so this data is processed and stored in the United States.
  • TelemetryDeck GmbH (Germany) processes the anonymous usage statistics above on our behalf.
  • Apple handles payments and, if you turn on the weather, the place lookup and weather request, under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

How long data is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it (Settings → Delete local data) or delete the app. Sleep tracker nights are limited to your last 60 nights; sound clips are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Usage statistics at TelemetryDeck: kept under TelemetryDeck's own retention rules (see its privacy policy).
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat: kept for as long as needed to provide and restore your subscription.
