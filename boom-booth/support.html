Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap "Step right up". Drag anywhere on the booth to aim (a dotted line shows the path) and let go to fire. While the ball is flying, tap once anywhere to make it go BOOM and knock down everything near it. Clear the booth with balls to spare to win up to three stars.

What are golden tickets?

You win them by hitting the bullseyes that fly across the booth, by knocking down golden cans, and with the first Daily Booth clear of each day. Spend them on the party popper, the water gun and the laser at the gun rack. Golden tickets cannot be bought.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Booth Blaster 1.1.0 has no ads and no in-app purchases. Every stage can be cleared with the starting cannon.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game never needs an internet connection, including the Daily Booth.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Use the Sound and Vibration switches at the bottom of the front screen. Sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch.
