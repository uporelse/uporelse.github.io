Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Aim and knock down the carnival bottles. Clear the rack to finish the level. You can watch an optional rewarded video for +3 balls or to skip a level when you choose.

Remove Ads ($2.99) and restoring a purchase

Boom Booth is free and shows ads. There is one optional one-time purchase, Remove Ads for $2.99, which removes the banner and full-screen ads. Optional rewarded videos you choose to watch still work. To buy or restore: open the menu and tap Remove ads or Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

Ads and child safety

The game is for a mixed audience, including kids, teens and adults. On first launch it asks an age range (Under 13, 13 to 15, 16 or older, or Not now). Players who say they are under 13, or skip the question, get child-directed, non-personalized ads limited to content rated G. Teens get the same non-personalized, G-rated treatment. Only players 16 or older may see Google's consent form where the law requires it, and Apple's tracking prompt only if they allow personalized ads. To report an inappropriate ad, email us at the address above.

How do I contact support?

Email ironclad.prod@gmail.com. We use your message only to reply.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game plays without an internet connection. Ads and purchases need a connection when they run.

My progress disappeared

Progress and Remove Ads status are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes local progress; use Restore purchases after reinstalling if you bought Remove Ads.
