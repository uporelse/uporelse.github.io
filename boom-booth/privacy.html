Boom Booth Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Boom Booth is a free carnival knock-down game for iPhone by Ironclad Productions. It is designed for a mixed audience, including children under 13, teens and adults. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. There is one optional in-app purchase, Remove Ads for $2.99, which removes the banner and full-screen ads and keeps the optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. There are no accounts.

What we (Ironclad Productions) collect

Nothing that identifies you. We have no servers for this game, no accounts and no analytics. Your progress (level, stars, settings, Remove Ads ownership flag, and your age-range answer) is saved only on your iPhone. Delete the app to erase it, or use Rostore purchases after reinstalling if you bought Remove Ads.

The age question

On first launch the app asks which age range applies: Under 13, 13 to 15 or 16 or older, or Not now. We do not ask for a birth date or name. Every answer plays the same game with the same rewards. The answer is stored only on your device and is used only to choose ad settings:/p>

  • Under 13, or Not now: ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent. Ads are non-personalized, limited to content rated G, and no consent form or tracking request is shown.
  • 13 to 15: ad requests use Google's teen treatment and are tagged under the age of consent. Ads are non-personalized and rated G. No consent form or tracking request is shown.
  • 16 or older: where the law requires it (for example the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), Google's consent form asks for your choices. Ads are still limited to content rated G. Only if you allow personalized ads in that form, the app then shows Apple's tracking permission request. Ads are personalized only if you allow both.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game decides when an ad may appear (a banner under the play area, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for +3 balls or to skip a level). When it loads ads, Google may collect and process, as described in Google's disclosure:

  • device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you, aged 16 or older, allowed tracking; otherwise app- or vendor-scoped identifiers);
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • product interaction, such as app launches and taps on ads;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services, under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. For children and teens Google limits this processing as described above. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

In-app purchases

Remove Ads is processed by Apple. Apple handles payment. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase status can be restored with Rostore purchases in the menu.

Consent and your choices

  • Tracking: IPhone Settings – Privacy & Security – Tracking.
  • Remove Ads or restore a prior purchase: menu – Remove ads / Restore purchases.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. For players who say they are under 13 or skip the question, ads are child-directed and non-personalized, and no consent or tracking requests are shown. Parents or guardians can contact us at ironclad.prod@gmail.com with any question or request.

If you email us

We receive your email address and message, use them only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed.
