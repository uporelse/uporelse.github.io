Boom Booth: Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 (version 1.1.0 and later). Boom Booth is a free carnival knock-down game for iPhone by Ironclad Productions.

What we collect

Nothing. Boom Booth has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK, never asks for tracking permission, and does not use the internet to play.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress is saved on your iPhone only: the stage you reached, your stars, golden tickets, the guns and prize balls you have won, your ribbons, your Daily Booth streak, and the sound and vibration switches. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

The Daily Booth

The Daily Booth is chosen on your iPhone from the calendar date. Nothing is downloaded and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects nothing, it collects no personal information from children.

Earlier versions

Version 1.0 of Boom Booth, which was never released on the App Store, contained Google AdMob ads and a Remove Ads purchase. Version 1.1.0 removed both.

Changes

If a later version adds advertising, purchases or any data collection, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
