Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Swipe through your camera roll month by month: left marks a photo for deletion, right keeps it, and you can undo. Marked items wait in the review bin until you tap Delete. Your photos never leave your iPhone.

I deleted something by mistake

Deleting always goes through Apple's own confirmation. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

The app only shows some of my photos

You may have given limited access. Tap Settings → Select more photos in the app, or change access in iOS Settings → Roll Diet → Photos.

What's free, and what's in Pro?

Free: swipe and delete in one month of your camera roll, your choice (you can switch the month until your first swipe in it). Roll Diet Pro unlocks every month and the smart piles (screenshots, bursts and look-alikes, large videos, old months). Pro is a yearly or monthly auto-renewing subscription. The price in your currency, and any free trial with the date it ends, are shown before you subscribe. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.

Why does "space freed" say "about"?

When iOS doesn't report an exact file size, the app estimates it, so the total is labelled as an estimate.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them with Apple:

  • On your iPhone: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Roll Diet → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or in the App Store app: tap your profile picture → Subscriptions → Roll Diet → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or in Roll Diet: Settings → Cancel anytime.

Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial or the current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one. After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are decided and paid by Apple, not by us (we can't see or refund your payment):

  • Sign in to https://reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you used to buy.
  • Tap I'd like to, then choose Request a refund.
  • Choose the reason, then Next.
  • Choose the Roll Diet subscription, then Submit.

Apple's help page: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

Restore purchases

In the app: Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Reset or delete my data

Settings → Reset progress clears your swipe choices and stats (your photos are not affected), or delete the app.
