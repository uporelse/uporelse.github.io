Roll Diet Support

Need help? Email ironclad.prod@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. You don't need an account, and we never ask for passwords.

How does it work?

Roll Diet sorts your camera roll into piles using rules you can read and change on the Rules screen: screenshots, bursts and look-alikes, long videos, old months, heavy days, trips, by kind, and piles you make yourself. Every item says why it is in its pile. Swipe left to mark an item, right to keep it, and undo anytime. Marked items wait in the review bin until you delete them.

Can I try it without giving photo access?

Yes. Tap "Try a sample roll first". The sample roll is drawn by the app, so you can try every pile, the burst compare and the review bin with no photo access. Nothing real is shown or deleted.

I deleted something by mistake

Deleting always goes through Apple's own confirmation. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can normally recover them for 30 days.

How do I keep something out of the piles?

Tap Keep forever on an item, or protect an album (for example Favorites) in Settings. Those items never show up in a pile.

The app only shows some of my photos

You may have given limited access. Tap Select more photos in the app, or change access in iOS Settings → Roll Diet → Photos.

What's free, and what's in Pro?

Free: every pile's rule, count and estimated size, changing the rules, making your own piles, clearing the Screenshots pile, one month of your choice (it locks after your first swipe in it) and the sample roll. Roll Diet Pro opens clearing every other pile and every month. Pro is yearly with a 7-day free trial, or monthly without a trial. The App Store price is shown before you subscribe, and subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.

Why are sizes "about"?

iOS does not give apps exact file sizes, so Roll Diet estimates them and labels them as estimates.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them with Apple: open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Roll Diet → Cancel Subscription, or in the App Store app tap your profile picture → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial or the current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are decided by Apple. Sign in to https://reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you used, choose Request a refund, and pick the Roll Diet subscription.

Restore purchases

On a new iPhone, open Settings in Roll Diet and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
