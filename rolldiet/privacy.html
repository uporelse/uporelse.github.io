Roll Diet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Roll Diet helps you swipe through your own photo library and delete what you no longer want. In short: your photos never leave your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What we do not collect

  • No photos, videos, thumbnails, file names, dates, places or any other photo detail is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No account or sign-up. No name, email, contacts or location.
  • No ads, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking. The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.

Photo library access

The app asks iOS for access to your photo library for two reasons only: to show you your own photos and videos so you can swipe through them, and to delete the ones you choose. You can give full or limited access, and change it anytime in iOS Settings → Roll Diet → Photos. The app does not ask to add photos and does not read photo locations.

The smart piles (screenshots, bursts and look-alikes, large videos, old months) are worked out on your iPhone from basic information iOS provides (date, size in pixels, video length, type). No image is sent to any server.

Deleting always goes through the iOS confirmation. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days and can be recovered.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (which items you kept or marked, your chosen free month and simple counts such as items reviewed) is saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. Clear it with Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app.

Share card

You can make an image that says roughly how much space you freed, with the app name and an App Store link. It shows numbers only, never your photos. It is made on your iPhone and only leaves it if you tap Share and choose where to send it.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder on your iPhone before the trial ends. There is no push server and no marketing notifications.

Purchases

Roll Diet Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Payment details are handled only by Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we never receive them. To check whether your subscription is active (including when you tap "Restore purchases"), the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as its purchase provider. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID created by the app, your App Store purchase/receipt information, and basic device info (such as iOS version and app version) needed to validate purchases. It does not receive your name, email or anything about your photos. This information is not linked to your identity by us and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). RevenueCat keeps purchase records under its own policy for as long as needed to provide and restore purchases.

Usage statistics

The current version of the app sends no usage statistics; Settings shows "This build sends no usage stats." The app has an optional feature for anonymous usage counts (through TelemetryDeck, https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy). If a future version turns it on, those counts will never include your photos or anything about them, will use a random ID created on your iPhone (not linked to you, not used for tracking), and can be turned off anytime in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. We will update this page before such a version is released.

Network

The only network requests the app makes are to Apple and RevenueCat for subscriptions and restore (and to iCloud through iOS if a photo is stored only in iCloud and you open it).

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Your choices

Because the app has no account and we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. Data on your iPhone can be deleted as described above. For purchase data, contact Apple or RevenueCat, or email us and we'll help.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

We never sell data

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
