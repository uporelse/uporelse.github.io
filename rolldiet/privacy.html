Roll Diet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 (version 1.1).

Roll Diet sorts your own camera roll into piles by rules you can read and change, so you can delete what you no longer want. It is built to work entirely on your iPhone.

Summary

  • Your photos never leave your iPhone. No photo, thumbnail, file name, date, place or any other photo detail is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No personal data. We do not collect, sell or share your name, email, contacts, location or anything that identifies you.
  • No analytics. The app has no analytics or usage tracking of any kind. The only thing that leaves your iPhone is the App Store purchase check for Pro, made through RevenueCat with an anonymous id. App Store label: Purchases, not linked to identity, not used for tracking.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. There is no sign-up, no ad SDK and no tracking of any kind (no IDFA, no ATT prompt, no data brokers).

Photo library access

The app asks iOS for access to your photo library for two reasons only: to show you your own photos and videos so you can swipe through them, and to delete the ones you choose. You can give full access or limited access (only the photos you pick), and change this anytime in iOS Settings → the app → Photos.

The piles (screenshots, bursts and look-alikes, long videos, old months, heavy days, trips, by kind and the piles you make yourself) are worked out on your iPhone from basic information iOS provides (date, size in pixels, video length, type). No image content is analysed by any server.

Deleting always goes through the iOS confirmation dialog. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days and can be recovered.

Data stored on your iPhone

The app stores your progress (which items you kept or marked, your chosen free month, your pile rules and own piles, your Keep forever list, protected album names, your storage goal, weigh-ins, diary and today's portion, and simple counts such as photos reviewed) in the app's local storage on your iPhone. It is not sent anywhere. Remove it with Settings → Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

Pile rules

The piles are built on your iPhone from plain rules you can read and change on the Rules screen. They use only the type, time, pixel size and length that iOS reports for each item. The app never analyzes what is in a picture.

Albums

If you choose albums to protect, the app reads which items are in those albums, on your iPhone, only to keep them out of the piles. Album names and contents are not sent anywhere.

Sample roll

The sample roll is a made-up camera roll drawn by the app. It contains none of your photos, needs no photo access, and clearing it deletes nothing on your iPhone.

Share card

You can share a card that shows numbers about your roll, such as how much space you freed or what share of its weight is video. It never contains a photo, a file name, a date or a place. The picture is made on your iPhone and goes only where you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Notifications

The app sends no notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. To check whether your subscription is active (including Restore purchases), the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID created by the app, your App Store purchase and receipt information, and basic technical data needed to validate purchases (such as iOS version, app version and IP address). It does not receive your name, email, Apple ID or anything about your photos, and it is not used for tracking or advertising. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Purchase history is handled by Apple and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies; we receive no personal data. Cancel anytime in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Network

The only network requests the app makes are to Apple and RevenueCat for subscriptions and restore (and to iCloud through iOS when a photo is stored only in iCloud and you open it).

Your choices

The app has no account and we hold no personal data about you. Data on your iPhone can be removed with Settings → Reset progress, or by deleting the app. For purchase data, contact Apple or RevenueCat, or email us and we will help.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
