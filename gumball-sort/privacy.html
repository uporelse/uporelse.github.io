Gumball Sort Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Gumball Sort is a free puzzle game for iPhone made by Ironclad Productions. It is designed for a mixed audience, including children under 13, teens and adults. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the app does not collect any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses, birth dates or ages are asked for.
  • No advertising. The app contains no ads and no advertising SDK.
  • No in-app purchases. Nothing in the app is for sale.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not show Apple's tracking permission prompt.
  • No chat, social features or user-generated content.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.
  • The game runs offline. It does not send your game data to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, unlocked themes, streaks, settings such as sound) is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. We cannot see it. It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

To delete it, open the menu and tap Reset all progress, or delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at ironclad.prod@gmail.com.

Links in the app

The menu has links to this page and to our support page. They open in your web browser. GitHub Pages hosts these pages; GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP address) when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed. We do not sell or share it.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
