Gumball Sort Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 (version 1.1). Gumball Sort: Crank & Jar is a puzzle game for iPhone by Ironclad Productions.

The short version

Gumball Sort collects no data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for permission to track you. On the App Store the privacy label for this version is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your iPhone

Your shop (the day you are on, stars, coins, upgrades, flavours, the ledger of jars sold, your best rush hour) and your settings (sound, vibration, marks on gumballs) are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. If you restore your iPhone from a backup, they come back with it.

No network use

The game is entirely inside the app and plays offline. The app does not contact our servers (we have none for this game) or any third-party service.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either. Parents or guardians can contact us with any question.

If you email us

We receive your email address and message, use them only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date before any change in how the app handles data.
