Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a tube to lift its top gumball, then tap another tube to drop it. A gumball can only go onto the same color or into an empty tube. Fill every tube with a single color to clear the level.

I'm stuck

Use Undo, Hint (shows a move that leads to a solution) or Tube (adds one empty tube for the level). Some uses cost coins you earn by clearing levels and from the daily gift. You can also restart the level at any time.

Does it cost anything? Are there ads?

No. Gumball Sort is free, has no ads and no in-app purchases. Coins are only earned in the game and cannot be bought.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game is inside the app and plays without an internet connection.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. If you restore your phone from a backup, progress from that backup comes back with it.

How do I start over?

Open the menu (top right) and tap Reset all progress.

Sound

Open the menu and tap Sound to turn sound effects on or off.
