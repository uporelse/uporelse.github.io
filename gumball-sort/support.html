Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Every gumball starts inside the machine. Tap the round Crank button and the next one drops into the tray; the window shows what comes after it. Tap a jar to drop the gumball in. Fill a jar with four of one colour and it is sold: a fresh empty jar takes its place and your shop earns coins. Tap one jar and then another to move a top gumball onto its own colour or into an empty jar. The day is sold out when the machine and every jar are empty.

What are the customers for?

A line of customers waits at the counter, each for a jar of one colour. Sell the jar the first one wants and they leave a tip. Any other jar still sells, just without the tip.

What is rush hour?

Rush hour opens on Day 4. The machine never runs dry, and a new colour joins every 10 jars you sell. Your score is how many jars you sell before the shop jams. Your best rush is kept in the shop.

I'm stuck

Use Undo (takes back a turn), Shake (mixes what is left in the machine, once a day) or Hint (shows a turn that still leads to selling out). If the shop jams you can take a move back, play the day again or go back to the shop. Every one of the first 80 days has been checked by a solver, so each one can be sold out.

Does it cost anything? Are there ads?

No. Jarbreaker has no ads and no in-app purchases. Coins are only earned by selling jars in the game and are only spent on shop upgrades and gumball flavours.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game is inside the app and never needs an internet connection.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. If you restore your phone from a backup, progress from that backup comes back with it.

Sound, vibration and colour marks

Scroll to Settings at the bottom of the shop screen to turn sound or vibration on or off, or to add a mark to each gumball colour for telling them apart.
