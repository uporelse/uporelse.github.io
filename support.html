Frequently asked questions

My alarm didn't ring

  • Open Settings → Up or Else in iOS and make sure Alarms is allowed.
  • Check that the alarm is switched on in the Alarms tab, and that your phone was on and charged.
  • Up or Else alarms use Apple's AlarmKit (iOS 26 or later) and ring even on Silent.

How do I stop an alarm?

Complete the alarm's mission: solve the math, shake the phone, type the phrase, answer the riddle, or scan your registered code. If you get stuck (3 wrong tries or about 60 seconds), the app switches to one easy task so you're never locked out.

What is "Are you really up?"

A few minutes after your mission, Up or Else can show one "I'm up" button. If you don't tap it within about a minute, the alarm rings again. You can switch it off per alarm.

How do streak freezes work?

You get one free freeze per week (Monday to Sunday). It's applied automatically to one missed alarm day so your streak continues.

Does the sleep tracker record me?

Yes, short clips, on your phone only. The sleep tracker listens through the microphone only while you run it, and analyses sound live on your iPhone. When it detects a sound, it saves a short clip (about 8–10 seconds) so you can play it back. Clips stay in the app's storage on your iPhone, are excluded from iCloud backup, and are never uploaded or sent to us or to analytics. There are at most about 30 clips per night, and they are deleted automatically after 7 days.

  • Play or delete one clip, a night's clips, or all clips in the sleep report.
  • To stop saving clips, turn off Settings → Save sound clips. The tracker still works without them.
  • Settings → Delete local data deletes all clips and your other data.

Why does the weather need my location?

Only if you turn on the weather (Settings → Morning card → Weather) and choose My location. The app then uses your approximate location, only while it is open, to get today's forecast from Apple Weather, which it shows on your alarm screen and on the Morning card. Prefer not to share your location? Choose A city in Settings → Weather and type a city instead; no location is used then. We never receive or store your location.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them with Apple:

  • On your iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Up or Else → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or in the App Store app: tap your profile picture → Subscriptions → Up or Else → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or in Up or Else: Settings → Manage subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one. After you cancel, your subscription stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and alarms you already set keep ringing after that.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are decided and paid by Apple, not by us (we can't see or refund your payment):

  • Sign in to https://reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  • Tap I'd like to, then choose Request a refund.
  • Choose the reason, then Next.
  • Choose the Up or Else subscription, then Submit.

Apple usually updates you within 24 to 48 hours. Apple's help page: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

My subscription ended. Do my alarms still work?

Yes. Alarms you already set keep ringing with their missions. Adding or editing alarms needs an active subscription.

Restore purchases

In the app: Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Delete my data

Everything is stored only on your phone. Settings → Delete local data removes it, or delete the app.
