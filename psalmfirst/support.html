Psalm First Support

Need help? Email ironclad.prod@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. There is no account, and we never ask for a password.

The daily lock

Psalm First can lock the apps, categories or websites you choose until you finish today’s reading. The lock uses Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) on your iPhone. The first time, iOS will ask you to allow Screen Time. If the lock does not start, open the app’s Settings and tap Allow Screen Time, then grant access when iOS asks.

You pick how firm the habit is: Gentle, Firm or Strict. On Firm and Strict, settings stay locked while the day’s shield is on, including during an emergency pass. Once each calendar day, an emergency pass opens the blocked apps for fifteen minutes without counting toward your streak.

Subscriptions

Psalm First Plus is optional. The daily lock and the free reading stay available without it. Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. The yearly plan includes a seven-day free trial. Prices and renewal terms are shown in the app before you buy. Restore purchases is on the Plus screen.

To change or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Or email us and we will point you there. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Optional tips

Support Psalm First in Settings lets you leave a one-time tip if you want to. Tips unlock no features; the app is the same with or without them.

Audio Bible

Reading always works offline. If you tap Listen, that chapter’s public-domain Berean Standard Bible recording plays from OpenBible.com over the internet.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy. Your readings, journal and settings stay on your iPhone. Delete the app to remove the data it stored on the device.
