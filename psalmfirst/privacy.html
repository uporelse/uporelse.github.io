Privacy Policy

Psalm First · Last updated November 13, 2026

Summary

Psalm First is made by Ironclad Productions. We collect no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no AI. Your readings, reflections, journal, streak and settings stay on your iPhone. Two things use the internet, and only when you choose them: purchase information for Plus, processed by Apple and RevenueCat (see Purchases), and the audio Bible (see Audio Bible).

Screen Time

The lock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) on your device. Apple gives the app opaque tokens for the apps you pick; we cannot see which apps they are and we never see your usage. Nothing about your app use leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether Plus is active, using an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts from Apple, not your name or email. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Audio Bible

Reading always works offline. If you tap Listen, the app plays that chapter's recording over the internet from OpenBible.com, which hosts the public domain audio of the Berean Standard Bible. Nothing is requested until you tap Listen. As with any website, that server receives your IP address and the file requested, which shows which chapter you played; the app sends it nothing else: no account, no identifier and nothing about your reading history. We do not receive or keep any record of what you listen to. See openbible.com for their own practices.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a local trial reminder on your device. No push server is used.

Children

Psalm First is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
