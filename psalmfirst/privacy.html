Privacy Policy

Psalms First · Last updated November 23, 2026

Summary

Psalms First is made by Ironclad Productions. We collect no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no AI. Your readings, reflections, journal, streak and settings stay on your iPhone. Three things use the internet, and only when you use them: purchase information for Plus, processed by Apple and RevenueCat (see Purchases); the audio Bible, when you tap Listen (see Audio Bible); and full-size encyclopedia pictures, which your iPhone fetches from Apple the first time you open them (see Pictures).

Screen Time

The lock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) on your device. Apple gives the app opaque tokens for the apps you pick; we cannot see which apps they are and we never see your usage. Nothing about your app use leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether Plus is active, using an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts from Apple, not your name or email. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Audio Bible

Reading always works offline. If you tap Listen, the app plays that chapter's recording over the internet from one of two sites: eBible.org, which hosts a free recording of the World English Bible, when the text on screen is the World English Bible; otherwise OpenBible.com, which hosts the public domain audio of the Berean Standard Bible. The line under the Listen button names the one in use. Nothing is requested until you tap Listen. As with any website, that server receives your IP address and the file requested, which shows which chapter you played; the app sends it nothing else: no account, no identifier and nothing about your reading history. We do not receive or keep any record of what you listen to. See openbible.com and ebible.org for their own practices.

Pictures

Every article, every thumbnail, the Bible and the map are inside the app. Most full-size encyclopedia pictures are not: Apple hosts them alongside the app as On-Demand Resources. Your iPhone fetches a small group of them from Apple the first time you open an entry that uses one, or all of them if you choose Download all pictures in Settings, and then keeps them on your iPhone (in the app's storage, not backed up to iCloud). The first download, and any later one if iOS has cleared the files to free space, needs a connection; without one, the thumbnail still shows. This is the same Apple service that delivers the app itself. We run no server for it, and no other company hosts the pictures. We receive nothing: no record of which pictures you opened and nothing about you.

Ratings

After you watch a History Map journey to the end, the app may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt, at most once every 120 days. Apple shows and handles the prompt; we receive only what Apple publishes on the App Store. The app keeps on your iPhone the date it last asked.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules local notifications on your device: the morning reading reminders you set in Reminders, and a trial reminder. They are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone; no push server is used.

Photos

If you choose Save Image or Save Video in the share sheet, the share card or map clip is added to your photo library on this iPhone. The app never reads your photo library, and the picture or video does not leave your device unless you share it yourself. Map clips record only this app's screen, with no sound and no microphone.

Children

Psalms First is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
