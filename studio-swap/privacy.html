Studio Swap Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Studio Swap is an iPhone app by Ironclad Productions. It is a cash-only marketplace for hiring paid help on game projects (micro-gigs). The app is for adults only (18+). There are no ads and no tracking.

What we (Ironclad Productions) collect

We do not run a server for this version of the app. Your account and project data are saved only on your iPhone. We have no analytics SDK and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Account and profile

When you create an account in the app, it stores on your device:

  • the display name you enter;
  • your role (hire help, sell skills, or both);
  • your date of birth (used only to check that you are 18 or older);
  • your confirmation that you are 18+;
  • when the account was created.

We do not ask for an email, phone number or payment card inside the app, and we do not receive those from the account screen.

Projects, messages and portfolio

Also stored only on your device:

  • briefs and gig orders you create (title, description, craft, platform, budget);
  • quotes, acceptance status, delivery notes and optional delivery links;
  • ratings and short review text you leave;
  • sellers you block and reports you file (reason and optional note).

Sample sellers shown in the app include demo portfolio links that open in your browser. Those listings are fictional demos. This version does not upload your own portfolio files to Ironclad Productions.

Purchases and RevenueCat

This version of Studio Swap does not process in-app purchases and does not send data to RevenueCat. Escrow, checkout and payout steps shown in the app are demos: no money moves and we do not receive payment card details. If a future version adds App Store purchases, they will be processed by Apple, and we may use RevenueCat with an anonymous app user ID and purchase history only (not your name or email). We will update this page before that ships.

Analytics and advertising

None. The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and it does not show Apple's tracking permission prompt because it does not track you.

Reports

Reports you file are saved on your device in this version. If you email us about a safety concern, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed.

Children

Studio Swap is 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor created an account, contact us and we will help remove the on-device data.

Deleting your account or data

Delete the app to erase the account, projects, briefs, reports and blocks stored on your device. You can also email ironclad.prod@gmail.com to request help deleting your data. Because this version keeps data only on your device, we cannot erase it remotely without your help.

If you email us

We receive your email address and message, use them only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed.

Links

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date before any change in how the app handles data.
