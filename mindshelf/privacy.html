Privacy Policy

Mind Shelf · Last updated November 9, 2026

Summary

Mind Shelf is made by Ironclad Productions. We do not run a server for the app, we do not track you and we show no ads. Your chats, what a twin remembers about you, your reports, blocks and any creator content you add stay on your device.

What we collect

No personal data. We have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising identifiers. AI twin replies are generated on your device from content the creator shared; your messages are not sent to an AI service. If you subscribe, Apple and RevenueCat process the purchase (purchase history and an anonymous app user ID, never your name or email).

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check which subscriptions are active, using an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts from Apple, not your name or email. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Reports and blocks

Reports and blocks are kept on your device in this version. If you email us about a safety or likeness concern, we use your email only to reply.

Children

Mind Shelf is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
