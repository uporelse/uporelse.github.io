Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Mole in the Room is an offline party game. We built it to collect as little as possible. In short: player names, secret words and your custom packs stay on your iPhone. The only thing that leaves the device is what Apple and our purchase provider need to unlock and restore packs you buy.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No ads, advertising identifiers (IDFA), crash reporting, analytics or tracking. The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We never collect player names, secret words or the contents of your custom packs. We do not sell or share data.

What stays on your phone

The player names you type, your settings and any custom word packs are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They never leave your phone. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Delete all data on this phone, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Buying an optional one-time pack or the Lifetime unlock goes through Apple's App Store. To check whether you've unlocked packs (including when you tap "Restore Purchases"), the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as its purchase provider. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID, your device's App Store receipt/transaction info, and basic device info (such as OS version and app version) needed to validate purchases. That information is not linked to your identity by us and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Payment details are handled only by Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Share cards

At the end of a round you can create a result card image. It is made on your phone and only leaves it if you tap Share and pick where to send it; we never post anything for you. The card includes the names and secret word from that round, so share it only with your group.

Network

Gameplay works fully offline. The app's network requests go to Apple and RevenueCat for purchases and restore. From version 1.0.3 the app also downloads seasonal word packs (a public word list) from uporelse.github.io; no personal data is sent.

Children

The game has family-friendly content and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds optional anonymous usage statistics), we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is released.
