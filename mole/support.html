Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

  1. Add everyone's names (3 to 20 players) and pick how many moles (1 to 3).
  2. Pass the phone. Each player peeks at their card, then hides it and passes it on.
  3. Talk it out before the timer ends.
  4. Secret vote, then the big reveal.

Full rules and scoring are in the app under How to play. Modes: Classic (the mole sees only the category), Undercover (the mole gets a similar word and isn't told) and Question (the mole gets a different question).

Do I need internet?

No. Gameplay works fully offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore packs.

What's free, and what can I buy?

The game and the Animals pack are free. Every other pack has a free demo round so you can try it first. You can buy packs one at a time, or get Lifetime, which unlocks every pack plus seasonal packs and your own custom packs. All purchases are one-time: no subscription, no free trial, nothing auto-renews. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you confirm.

Restore my purchases

In the app: Settings → Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are decided and paid by Apple, not by us (we can't see or refund your payment):

  • Sign in to https://reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you used to buy.
  • Tap I'd like to, then choose Request a refund.
  • Choose the reason, then Next.
  • Choose the Mole in the Room purchase, then Submit.

Apple usually updates you within 24 to 48 hours. If you're in the EU/EEA, your 14-day right of withdrawal for the purchase is also handled by Apple this way. Apple's help page: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

Are custom packs shared with anyone?

No. Custom packs are typed on your phone and stored only there.

Delete my data

Everything is stored only on your phone. Settings → Delete all data on this phone removes player names, settings and custom packs, or delete the app. Your purchases stay with your Apple ID and can be restored.
