TimeBloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

TimeBloom is a calm, visual day planner. In short: your plans stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What we do not collect

  • Nothing you plan: we have no server that receives your blocks, routines, Inbox items, subtasks or energy/mood check-ins.
  • No account or sign-up. No name, email, contacts or location.
  • No ads, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking. The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your blocks, routines, Inbox, subtasks, check-ins and settings are stored only in the app's storage on this iPhone. The Home Screen widget and Live Activity (Pro) read a small summary of your current and next blocks from a shared container on this iPhone; it is not uploaded.

Settings → Delete all my data erases your plans, routines, Inbox, check-ins, settings and the widget summary from this iPhone after you confirm. Deleting the app does too. Your purchase is not affected: it stays with your Apple ID.

Calendar (Pro, optional)

If you choose "Add today's calendar events", iOS asks for calendar access. The app reads today's timed events on this iPhone only to show them on your timeline. It never changes, creates, deletes or uploads calendar events. You can turn access off anytime in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Notifications are local, scheduled on this iPhone (no push server). They are used for start/end reminders of your own blocks (turn them off in Settings) and, if you start a free trial and allow it, one reminder before the trial ends. No marketing notifications.

Share my day

"Share my day" makes an image of today's timeline on this iPhone. By default it shows only colours and times; block titles appear only if you turn on "Show block titles". It only leaves your iPhone if you share it.

Purchases

TimeBloom Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Payment details are handled only by Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we never receive them. To check whether your subscription is active (including when you tap "Restore purchases"), the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as its purchase provider. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID created by the app, your App Store purchase/receipt information, and basic device info (such as iOS version and app version) needed to validate purchases. It does not receive your name, email or anything you plan. This information is not linked to your identity by us and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). RevenueCat keeps purchase records under its own policy for as long as needed to provide and restore purchases.

Usage statistics

The current version of the app sends no usage statistics; Settings shows "This build sends no usage stats." The app has an optional feature for anonymous usage counts (through TelemetryDeck, https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy). If a future version turns it on, those counts will never include your task text, times, routines, check-ins or calendar, will use a random ID created on your iPhone (not linked to you, not used for tracking), and can be turned off anytime in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. We will update this page before such a version is released.

Not a medical product

TimeBloom is a planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Your choices

Because the app has no account and we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. Data on your iPhone can be deleted as described above. For purchase data, contact Apple or RevenueCat, or email us and we'll help.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

We never sell data

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
