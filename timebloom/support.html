Frequently asked questions

What's free, and what's in Pro?

The core planner is free: the Today timeline, quick add and edit, steps (subtasks), the Inbox, auto-shift of unfinished blocks, gentle start/end reminders and up to 2 routines. TimeBloom Pro adds calendar import (read-only, on this iPhone), the Home Screen widget and current-block Live Activity, unlimited routines, the focus timer and energy/mood check-ins. Pro is a yearly or monthly auto-renewing subscription. The price in your currency, and any free trial with the date it ends, are shown before you subscribe. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.

My reminders don't show up

In the app, check Settings → Gentle reminders. In iOS, open Settings → Notifications → TimeBloom and make sure notifications are allowed.

Calendar events don't appear

Calendar import is a Pro feature. Tap Settings → Add today's calendar events and allow calendar access. It reads today's timed events on this iPhone only and never changes your calendar.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them with Apple:

  • On your iPhone: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → TimeBloom → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or in the App Store app: tap your profile picture → Subscriptions → TimeBloom → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or in TimeBloom: Settings → Manage or cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial or the current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one. After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are decided and paid by Apple, not by us (we can't see or refund your payment):

  • Sign in to https://reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account you used to buy.
  • Tap I'd like to, then choose Request a refund.
  • Choose the reason, then Next.
  • Choose the TimeBloom subscription, then Submit.

Apple's help page: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

Restore purchases

In the app: Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Delete my data

Your plans are stored only on your iPhone. Settings → Delete all my data erases them and starts the app fresh, or delete the app. Your purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored.

Is TimeBloom a medical product?

No. TimeBloom is a planning tool. It is not a medical product and does not give medical advice.
